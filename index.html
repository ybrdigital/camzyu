Universal DirectShow Webcam

Remote Studio Controls

Synchronized Audio

High-fidelity 48 kHz stereo microphone capture with microsecond timestamp synchronization feeding directly into your PC's audio devices and video calls.

Hardware-Accelerated Encoding

Leverages Android MediaCodec hardware encoders (H.264 / HEVC) paired with native FFmpeg decoding on Windows for optimal CPU performance.

Getting Started

Quick Installation Guide

Follow these simple steps to install Camybr and use it across your favorite video tools.

  1. Download the Windows installer: Get Camybr-Windows-v1.0.0-Setup.exe from above.
  2. Run the Camybr setup wizard: Follow the on-screen prompts to install the virtual camera and audio drivers.
  3. Open your video app: Launch OBS Studio, Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet, Discord, or Skype.
  4. Select Camybr: In your app settings, choose Camybr as your Camera and Camybr Audio as your Microphone.
  5. Connect your Android device: Start streaming in the Camybr Android app over 5 GHz Wi-Fi or USB tethering to transmit your live camera feed!

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Camybr, supported video tools, and features.

Camybr is a high-performance streaming camera solution that turns your Android smartphone into a studio-grade PC webcam and microphone. It captures hardware-accelerated video (up to 4K 60 FPS) and synchronized audio, transmitting them directly over 5 GHz Wi-Fi or wired USB to Windows PC—compatible with OBS Studio, Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet, Discord, Skype, and any desktop video app.

Yes! The Camybr Windows Plugin is 100% free software (freeware, no watermark on PC). The companion Camybr Android mobile application is available on Google Play with optional Pro upgrades for creators desiring 4K UHD and 60 FPS studio presets.

Camybr installs a standard Windows DirectShow camera and audio driver, making it fully compatible with OBS Studio (28.0 through 31.0+), Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet, Discord, Skype, Streamlabs Desktop, vMix, XSplit, Webex, and all web browsers (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Brave) on Windows 10 and Windows 11 (64-bit).

Yes! Camybr supports wired USB connections via standard Android USB Tethering. Simply enable USB tethering in your phone settings, and Camybr auto-detects the wired high-speed connection for minimum latency and zero packet loss.

Yes! Camybr connects over local Wi-Fi networks. We strongly recommend a 5 GHz Wi-Fi connection for stable 1080p and 4K 60 FPS streaming. The plugin includes automatic UDP broadcast discovery on port 4750 to detect your phone automatically.
